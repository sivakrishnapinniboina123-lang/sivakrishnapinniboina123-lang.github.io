Flagship Project

Flywheel Power Generation System for EVs - Extending Range by 30-40%

Designed and analyzed a flywheel-based regenerative system that captures kinetic energy during braking and reuses it. The system operates at 916 RPM (optimal for energy recovery) and reduces battery load, extending EV range.

Key Highlights:

  • Working Principle: Kinetic Energy Recovery using Flywheel (½ I ω²)
  • Design Speed: 916 RPM optimized for city driving cycles
  • Software Used: CATIA V5, ANSYS, SolidWorks
  • Result: 30-40% theoretical range extension, reduced battery degradation
  • Applications: Electric Vehicles, Hybrid Systems, Sustainable Mobility
